# Dead Air transmissions

Twenty original starting points for familiar vocal-effect styles. These are designed for Dead Air, not measured JMT hardware settings or a popularity ranking. Source level and vocal pitch affect the result; adjust Damage and Carrier to suit the recording. Low-carrier growls use modulation and do not transpose the voice down an octave. No factory preset needs external routing or uses Mute.

Choose a Transmission to recall every control and its mode. Asterisk (*) means the selected voice has been edited. Earlier sessions retain their existing controls and display Unknown transmission. Switching modes manually keeps your Full-mode controls; loading a new preset replaces the complete settings as expected.

Preset choices are also exposed as DAW programs. Preset identity, edited controls and mode are saved with the session. The original plug-in IDs and parameter IDs remain unchanged for existing projects.
